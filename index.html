Welcome to the Warriors Wiki!

This is a clone of the Fandom Wiki for the Warriors series by Erin Hunter. It was founded on June 21st, 2006. It has 5,713 articles, 1,951,951 edits, and currently 117 active editors. It's goal is to create a quality database for the series in its entirety. Anyone can be an editor. If you want to start editing the wiki, check out the wiki rules and the community tab, and contact a staff member if you have any questions.


Newest Release: Chasing Shadows

Moonpaw finally confesses to her Clanmates that her dead sister has been torturing her in her mind. But as Moonpaw grows closer to Goldenpaw, her sister's threats become more ominous, and Moonpaw worries she's a danger to her entire Clan. Tawnypelt is sure that only the elders can see the Clans through the oncoming danger. Starlingpaw grows bolder in his belief that he alone must save the Clans. But can an apprentice really hold the key to their salvation?
