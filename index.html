Welcome to the Warriors Wiki!

This is a clone of the Fandom Wiki for the Warriors series by Erin Hunter. It was founded on June 21st, 2006. It has 5,713 articles, 1,951,951 edits, and currently 117 active editors. It's goal is to create a quality database for the series in its entirety. Anyone can be an editor. If you want to start editing the wiki, check out the wiki rules and the community tab, and contact a staff member if you have any questions.
